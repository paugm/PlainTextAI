A hobby experiment

Train a tiny model on a text you pick.

You give this page a .txt file, or one of the books already sitting here. It reads the words and remembers which ones tend to come next. Then you type a starting line and it tries to keep going.

It is a toy. The sentences wander a lot. You can peek at why it picked each word if you want.

Your file stays on this computer.
